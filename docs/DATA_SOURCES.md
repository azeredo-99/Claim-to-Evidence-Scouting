# Data sources

| Source | Used for | Conditions | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FotMob** | Season totals for the subject and league peers; team possession; the subject's past seasons (D6) | Collected manually at each snapshot. Raw snapshots are kept out of git. "Data: FotMob" on every figure and report | Field check pending |
| **slgr.gr** (official league site) | Starts, minutes, substitutions, cards | Manual consultation of match sheets; terms to be read | Pending field check |
| **FBref** | Cross-check of minutes and goals | Terms of Use clause 5 allows reuse with citation | Optional |
| **Own video coding** | C04, C09, C13, D2, D4 | Own data; only legitimately accessible matches; no images or clips published | Protocol drafted |
| **Synthetic data** | Demo and tests | Generated by `scripts/make_synthetic.py`; fictional players and teams | Available |

Not used: StatsBomb Open Data and football-data.org (no coverage of this league), Sofascore and
Transfermarkt (terms do not allow extraction).
